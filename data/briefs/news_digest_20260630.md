**UK FCA Publishes Final Crypto Rules, Licensing Regime to Begin October 2027**
The UK Financial Conduct Authority released final crypto regulations covering exchanges, custodians, stablecoin issuers, and staking providers—all must obtain FCA authorization to operate. Firms can apply from September 30, 2026, with mandatory compliance starting October 25, 2027. The framework includes capital and stress-test requirements, market abuse rules, and stablecoin standards; key capital requirements for stablecoin issuers were reduced from 2% to 1%, and retail clients gain access to the Financial Ombudsman Service for complaints.

**Australia's Crypto Travel Rule Takes Effect July 1**
Starting July 1, Australia's crypto travel rule—enforced by AUSTRAC—requires users of locally regulated exchanges to provide additional information for both incoming and outgoing transfers, including sender/recipient names and platform details. The rule aims to improve crypto transfer traceability and reduce money laundering, terrorist financing, and scam risks; self-custody wallet transfers also require ownership confirmation.
