🏛️ Regulation & Policy

UK Finalizes Sweeping Crypto Framework Ahead of 2027 Licensing Rollout
The UK's Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) published final crypto asset rules covering capital requirements, market abuse standards, and stablecoin provisions—set to take effect October 25, 2027. Exchanges, custodians, stablecoin issuers, and staking services must obtain FCA authorization; the application window runs September 30, 2026 to February 28, 2027. The regime lowers stablecoin capital buffers from 2% to 1% and extends consumer-complaint rights to retail clients, marking a milestone for regulatory clarity in a major jurisdiction.

Australia's Crypto Travel Rule Takes Effect July 1
AUSTRAC's new travel-rule requirements mandate that users of locally regulated exchanges provide sender/recipient names and platform details for all transfers—including to self-custody wallets—in a bid to improve AML traceability and reduce scam risks. The move aligns Australia with global FATF standards and tightens onramps for illicit finance.
