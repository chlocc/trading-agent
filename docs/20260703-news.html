Brazil Central Bank Places Crypto Asset Service Providers Under Securities-Level Supervision
Brazil's central bank issued Decision 580/2026, placing Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) under Type 3 regulatory supervision—the same prudential framework as securities brokers and forex dealers. Starting January 1, 2027, VASPs must comply with risk management, capital adequacy, and disclosure requirements, with all providers moving to Segment 4 supervision by June 30, 2028. The measure follows the principle of "same business, same risk, same regulation" and aims to align Brazil's crypto oversight with international standards.
