IMF Paper: Dollar Stablecoins Widen FX Access But Risk Coordinated Runs
An IMF working paper by Brandon Tan finds that dollar stablecoins expand foreign exchange access in markets with restricted official dollar channels, but can coordinate destabilizing runs on pegged currencies.
