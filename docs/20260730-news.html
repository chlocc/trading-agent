Australia Sues Telegram Over Alleged Failure to Remove Pro-Terror Content, Seeks A$54.6M Penalty
Australia's eSafety regulator has filed a civil lawsuit against Telegram for systematically violating the Online Safety Act by failing to remove flagged pro-terror content, including execution videos from the 2019 Christchurch mosque shooting and 2022 Buffalo mass shooting. The regulator alleges that some content remained accessible for nearly three weeks after being reported and that Telegram failed to enforce uniform service terms against terrorism-related material. Under Australian law, violations carry maximum civil penalties of A$54.6 million (approximately $36 million USD).

Polymarket to Upgrade Crypto Price Settlement Rules with Time-Weighted Average Pricing
Polymarket announced it will overhaul settlement mechanisms for crypto price fluctuation markets starting August 7, abandoning single-price snapshots in favor of time-weighted average price (TWAP) windows. All crypto 5-minute markets will use 30-second TWAP, 15-minute markets will use 60-second TWAP, and 4-hour markets will also use 60-second TWAP.
