U.S. and Japan Conduct Rare Joint FX Intervention to Support Yen
The U.S. Treasury and Bank of Japan executed their first coordinated currency intervention since 2011, with Japan deploying an estimated $34 billion on Friday alone to stabilize the yen after it hit 164 per dollar—its weakest level since 1986. This marks the first joint action specifically targeting yen support since 1998 and reflects mounting concerns over inflationary pressure from currency weakness, though the move also underscores geopolitical coordination at a time of heightened global volatility.

The European Commission gained new AI regulatory enforcement powers effective August 2, enabling it to assess general-purpose AI (GPAI) models, restrict market access, and impose fines up to €15 million or 3% of global annual revenue—whichever is higher—on companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The move is seen as a key step in the EU's push for "technological sovereignty," though it risks further escalating transatlantic tech friction following prior clashes over the Digital Markets Act.
