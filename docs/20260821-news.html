CFTC Chair: Crypto Market Structure Rules Coming Regardless of Legislative Delays
CFTC Chairman Michael Selig stated that the crypto industry will receive market structure rules even if Congress fails to pass the Clarity Act. Selig emphasized the agency has authority under existing laws to establish regulations if legislative efforts stall, and the CFTC is working to finalize rules for prediction markets. The Clarity Act, which could give the CFTC primary oversight of digital assets, has faced Senate delays over ethics provision disputes.
