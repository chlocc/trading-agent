Pakistan Launches Virtual Asset Licensing Regime with September 5 Deadline
Pakistan's Virtual Asset Regulatory Authority (PVARA) officially launched the country's crypto licensing system, requiring digital asset companies to submit no-objection certificate applications by September 5 or cease operations. Licensed entities must comply with strict operational and security standards including customer fund safety, robust cybersecurity, clear disclosure, and transparent business practices. The licensing rules cover 11 crypto-related activities including custody, trading platforms, brokerage, and derivatives.
