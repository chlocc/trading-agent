Las Vegas businessman convicted in $24M crypto fraud, faces up to 280 years in prison
Brent Kovar was found guilty by a federal jury for operating a cryptocurrency Ponzi scheme through Profit Connect, defrauding at least 400 investors of approximately $24 million between 2017 and 2021. He falsely promised fixed annual returns of 20-30% from blockchain mining and AI-powered "supercomputers," but instead used new funds to pay earlier investors and transferred millions into his mother's personal accounts. His sentencing is scheduled for November 30, marking one of the more severe fraud prosecutions in the crypto space.
