California Passes Bill Banning Politicians' Meme Coins, Awaits Governor Signature
California's AB 2409, which would prohibit public officials and government employees from issuing meme coins, has passed both state houses and awaits the governor's signature. Starting January 1, 2027, digital asset service providers would also be barred from listing meme coins issued by federal, state, or local officials to California residents. If signed, this would become one of the first state-level regulations in the U.S. targeting political figures' meme coin issuance.
