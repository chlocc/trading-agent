U.S. Court Reinstates State Authority Over Kalshi Sports Contracts, Setting Up Supreme Court Battle
The Ninth Circuit ruled that Nevada has the right to regulate Kalshi's sports event contracts as gambling, rejecting Kalshi's argument that CFTC has exclusive federal jurisdiction. The decision conflicts with a separate federal appellate ruling earlier this year that sided with Kalshi, creating a circuit split that may need Supreme Court resolution. Kalshi says it will seek further review, while state regulators argue sports contracts are inherently gambling products.

BIS Chief: Stablecoins Lack Credibility for Large-Scale Payments, Tokenized Deposits Preferable
BIS General Manager Pablo Hernández de Cos told the Jackson Hole symposium that stablecoins are not a credible means of payment at scale and that tokenized bank deposits offer a more compelling path for leveraging blockchain technology. He noted stablecoins could lower government borrowing costs by boosting demand for U.S. Treasuries, but warned that mass migration of bank deposits into stablecoins could raise lending costs for households and firms. BIS research simultaneously highlighted significant regulatory divergence across the U.S., EU, UK, Hong Kong, and Singapore.

Polygon PoS Nodes Fall Out of Consensus After Austin and Kyoto Hard Forks
Polygon Labs issued an emergency notice that nodes still running old versions of Bor or Heimdall have split from consensus following the Austin (block 91,949,700) and Kyoto (block 51,533,000) hard forks. Operators must upgrade to Bor v2.10.0+ and Heimdall v0.11.0+ immediately. The Austin fork patched two resource-exhaustion vulnerabilities, including an L1-to-L2 bridge sync event that could delay block processing and an unbounded TxDependency field that could crash peer nodes.
