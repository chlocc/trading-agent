Singapore MAS Proposes Stablecoin Licensing Framework With 100% Reserve Requirement
The Monetary Authority of Singapore has proposed amendments to the Payment Services Act creating a dedicated stablecoin issuance license, requiring 100% reserve backing, redemption deadline compliance, and a ban on paying interest to holders. Only licensed issuers could label tokens "MAS-regulated stablecoins," with quarterly stress tests also mandated. Public consultation closes October 16, making this one of the most detailed stablecoin frameworks globally.
